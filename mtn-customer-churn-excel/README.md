# MTN Customer Churn Analysis in Excel

I used Excel to explore customer churn in an MTN customer dataset. I wanted to understand the reported reasons for leaving, how churn related to satisfaction, and how customer segments contributed to recorded revenue.

## Analysis covered

- Overall customer counts and churn
- Reported reasons for churn
- Revenue by churn status
- Churned customer counts by satisfaction score and location
- Leading subscription plans by revenue rank

## Key findings

The supplied analysis covers **484 unique customers** for Nigeria in **Q1 2026**. It records **141 churned customers**, giving an overall churn rate of **29.13%**, and **343 retained customers**.

Recorded revenue totals **₦199,348,200**, equivalent to approximately **₦411.88 thousand per customer**. Better competitor offers are the most frequently reported churn reason, accounting for **28 of the 141 churned customers**.

## Interpreting the results

The satisfaction and state charts show customer counts, rather than segment churn rates. Comparing the likelihood of churn across segments requires the total customer count in each segment.

Imo has the largest churned customer count among the labeled states shown, with **9 customers**. The state chart includes only 18 states with visible names and is not a complete ranking.

Churned customers account for **29.1% of recorded revenue**. This is historical revenue associated with those customers, not an estimate of future revenue lost.

Subscription plan names are abbreviated in the supplied material. The presentation shows their revenue rank, but exact plan revenue values are unavailable.
